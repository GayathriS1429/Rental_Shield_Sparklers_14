RentalShield EDA outputs
========================
Start with eda_summary.csv.
crime_duplicate_audit_by_file.csv flags suspected exact repeats by source file; no crime records are automatically removed.
crime_exact_duplicate_rows_for_review.csv contains repeated records after the first occurrence for manual review.
Police incident counts are not population-adjusted crime rates. 2026 may be a partial year.
Privacy-protected locations cannot support address-level matching.
Rent benchmarks are average asking rents; check raw estimate/unavailable markers.
CAFC general fraud statistics do not prove rental-deposit fraud at an individual address.
These exploratory results do not prove a property is safe or fraudulent.
